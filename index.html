Abstract

Mamba-family forecasters absorb non-stationary confounding events (unrecorded irrigation booms, dam-operation shifts, sensor recalibrations) into their state-transition matrices and keep forward-propagating the bias long after the physical cause ends. SSU-LSF localizes a temporal confounding footprint with curvature-weighted influence scores and removes it by Hessian-free projected gradient ascent inside a KL-divergence trust region with spatial total-variation regularization, at a fraction of the cost of full retraining.

Method

1 · Influence
EKFac curvature-preconditioned per-step scores φt on the confounded model (Eq. 4).
2 · Footprint Φ
The confounding window plus a geometric tail of half-width 0.5ρ/(1−ρ).
3 · Unlearn
Ascent on Φ, clean rehearsal, KL trust region, spatial TV on the location embeddings (Eq. 5).

Compact configuration — python run_experiments.py --compact (36 locations, 72 steps, ~1 min): GR CRR 0.946 at 17.1% clean-domain damage, SSU-LSF CRR 0.696 at 3.8%, ρ_max = 0.906, confounding retained 64.5%, TCI 0.625 / 0.679 / 0.636 / 0.639, SCS 0.1665 / 0.1671 / 0.1654 / 0.1658.

This reproduces the confounding gap that keeps CRR meaningful, ρ_max, the footprint tail, gradient reversal's 7–22% clean-domain damage (20.5% here, 17.1% compact), SSU-LSF's clean-domain advantage (−1.7% here against the 4.2% worst case), and the TCI and SCS orderings of Table 1. It does not reproduce the CRR ordering between SSU-LSF and GR: the KL trust region saturates SSU-LSF after 30 accepted steps (CRR 0.569 at ΔRMSE 4.8%, unchanged at 45–90 epochs), while the unconstrained GR arm reaches 0.655 at 15 epochs and 0.947 at 30 epochs with 81% clean-domain damage. The archive here is synthetic, the backbone is a compact selective diagonal SSM rather than the 47.3M Mamba encoder-decoder, and the curvature is a damped diagonal rather than EKFac, so these tables are a mechanism-level check of the pipeline; docs/REPRODUCE.md lists the paper-scale settings and docs/VERIFICATION.md the full comparison.
