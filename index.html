SSU-LSF
State-Space Unlearning for Non-Stationary Bias in Land Surface Forecasting

Forget the anomaly. Keep the climate.

Abstract

Mamba-family forecasters absorb unrecorded events (irrigation booms, dam-operation shifts, sensor recalibrations) into their state matrices and keep propagating the bias after the cause ends. SSU-LSF localizes a temporal footprint with curvature-weighted influence scores and removes it by projected gradient ascent inside a KL trust region with spatial TV regularization, at a fraction of the cost of full retraining.

Method

1 · Influence
EKFac-preconditioned per-step scores φt on the confounded model.
2 · Footprint Φ
Window plus a geometric tail of half-width 0.5ρ/(1−ρ).
3 · Unlearn
Ascent on Φ, clean rehearsal, KL trust region, spatial TV.
